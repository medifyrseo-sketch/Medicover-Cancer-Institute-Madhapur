# Medicover Cancer Institute Madhapur, Hyderabad

[Medicover Cancer Institute Madhapur](https://medifyr.com/hyderabad/hospital/medicover-cancer-institute-madhapur) is a specialized cancer care centre located in HITEC City, Madhapur, Hyderabad. Part of the Medicover Hospitals network, the institute provides comprehensive oncology services with a focus on cancer diagnosis, treatment, surgery, and patient care. It caters to patients from Hyderabad and surrounding regions, offering access to multiple oncology specialties under one roof.

## Why Choose Medicover Cancer Institute Madhapur?

The institute focuses on coordinated cancer treatment through a multidisciplinary team of medical professionals. Its oncology specialists work across different departments to evaluate cases and develop treatment plans based on individual patient requirements. Patients can consult specialists for cancer diagnosis, treatment options, second opinions, and follow-up care.

## Departments and Medical Specialties

Medicover Cancer Institute offers several specialized departments, including:

- Medical Oncology
- Surgical Oncology
- Radiation Oncology
- Hemato-Oncology
- Breast Oncology
- Uro-Oncology
- Thoracic Oncology
- Nuclear Medicine
- Neurosurgery
- Surgical Gastroenterology

These departments support the evaluation and management of different types of cancers and related medical conditions.

## Cancer Treatments and Procedures

The hospital provides various cancer treatment services, including chemotherapy, radiation therapy, cancer surgeries, and other specialized procedures. Available treatments include breast cancer treatment, liver cancer treatment, blood cancer treatment, brain tumour surgery, and throat cancer surgery. Procedures such as Transarterial Radioembolization (TARE), Transarterial Chemoembolization (TACE), and Bone Marrow Transplant are also listed among its services.

## Advanced Facilities and Technology

The institute highlights several diagnostic and treatment facilities to support cancer care. These include 4D PET-CT scanning, MRI, X-ray, mammography, ultrasound, chemotherapy day-care units, intensive care services, and modern treatment infrastructure. Additional facilities include pharmacy services, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and inpatient and outpatient care.

## Experienced Doctors and Patient Care

Medicover Cancer Institute has a team of specialists across medical oncology, surgical oncology, radiation oncology, and other clinical departments. Patients can explore available doctors, review their qualifications and experience, and select specialists according to their medical requirements. The hospital also emphasizes coordinated care and communication throughout the treatment journey.
